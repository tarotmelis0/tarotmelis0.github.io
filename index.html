Tarot Melis

Daily tarot readings and card meanings in short videos.

What is Tarot Melis?

Tarot Melis is a personal content tool used by the creator of the Tarot Melis TikTok account. It prepares short vertical videos about tarot cards, daily readings and card meanings, and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok account at the times the creator chooses.

How it works

  • The creator writes a video topic (for example, "The meaning of The Star card").
  • The tool writes a short script, creates illustrations and a voice-over, and assembles a video with subtitles.
  • The creator can watch, edit the caption and approve the video before it is posted.
  • After the creator logs in with TikTok and gives permission, the video is posted to the creator's own account on the chosen schedule.

TikTok integration

Tarot Melis uses TikTok Login Kit to connect the creator's own account and the Content Posting API to publish videos. It only posts to the account that authorized it, and the creator can disconnect at any time. Videos are labeled as AI-generated content.
