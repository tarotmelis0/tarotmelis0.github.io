Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Tarot Melis (the "Service") handles and how it is used. The Service is a personal tool used by the owner of the Tarot Melis TikTok account to create and publish videos to that account.

1. Information we receive from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, we receive only what you authorize:

  • Basic profile information (scope user.info.basic): your open ID, display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens: used only to publish videos you have prepared to your own account (scope video.publish) and to check whether a post was published successfully.

We do not access your followers, messages, private videos, contacts or any other data.

2. How the information is used

  • To display the connected account in the Service.
  • To upload and publish videos to the connected account at the times you choose.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell, rent or share it with third parties.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and settings are stored locally on the device where the Service runs. They are not uploaded to any server operated by us. Videos are sent directly to TikTok through TikTok's official API.

4. Content generation services

To create videos, video topics are sent to AI services (for writing scripts, creating illustrations and generating voice-over). Only the topic text and generated script are sent; no TikTok account data is shared with these services.

5. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time from the Service; this deletes the stored tokens and profile information and revokes the Service's access. You can also remove access from your TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. To request deletion of any remaining data, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: hhgffjsjj@gmail.com
